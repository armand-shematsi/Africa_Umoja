Services

What we take on

From the first conduit on a new wall to a socket that has to work tonight.

01

House wiring

Cable and conduit for houses still in block and mortar, run so the plaster can close over a straight line.

02

Lighting

Ceiling spots, shop floors, and rooms that need light in the right place once the ceiling is finished.

03

Sockets and switches

Set level, earthed, and covered. The part of the installation a household actually touches.

04

Earthing

Pits and bonding, so a fault has a safe path into the ground instead of into a person.

05

Fault finding

Dead sockets, tripped boards, burnt points, and circuits that cut out for no clear reason.

06

Shops and small power

Fit-outs for a trading space, plus supply for cameras and other small equipment.

The crew

One standard, from the pit to the switch.

Umoja means unity. On site it means the same care whether the wall is still block or the ceiling is already painted.

The people in these photos pull the cable, fit the points, and stand in front of the work. Helmets, glasses, gloves, and boots stay on. The installation should be the quiet part of the building: safe to forget about, because it was done properly.
